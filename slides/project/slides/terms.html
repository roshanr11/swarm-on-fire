KEY TERMS, IN PLAIN WORDS

Eight words you’ll hear

Operator

The company that deploys a group of agents and writes their instructions.

Front

An account posing as independent, secretly run by the same hidden boss as others.

Honest twins

Two truly independent operators who happen to share an opinion.

Alias

A username. Every rename starts a new one.

Gateway

The server every AI call passes through. It can stamp an ID the agent can’t change.

Fingerprint

A profile built only from what an account writes: its style, or its meaning.

AUC score

How well a method tells matches from non-matches. 0.5 = coin flip, 1.0 = perfect.

F1 score

How accurately posts are traced to the real boss. 0 = not at all, 1 = perfect.
