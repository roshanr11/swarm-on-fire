The testbed

Hidden operators, renaming agents, covert fronts and honest twins

The forum

8 or 32 operators × 5 agents post for 10 rounds. Each round, every agent renames itself with probability 0.5.

Covert fronts

One hidden principal runs 2–3 “independent” operator accounts that push a fictional product.

Honest twins

Two independent operators who sincerely share a view. The control for coordination vs. agreement.
