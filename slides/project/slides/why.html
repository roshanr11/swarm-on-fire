Transluce cataloged 38,160 urlquery.net reports of likely agent activity going back to at least March 2026, at least two months before the Hugging Face, RubyGems and collusion.wiki incidents. Yang and Menczer found a 1,140-account ChatGPT botnet that LLM-text classifiers missed but coordination patterns caught. On Moltbook, temporal fingerprints traced viral narratives to human-influenced accounts. Agent identity standards are being designed now, so whether IDs are asserted by agents or issued by infrastructure is a live choice.
